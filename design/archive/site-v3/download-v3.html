    05 Good to know

    Updates you approve

    Rift checks for new versions, verifies the signature, and installs only when you say so. Stable or beta channel.

    Portable mode

    The portable builds keep every setting in a Data folder beside the app. Carry it on a USB drive.

    Clean uninstall

    Removing Rift removes its shortcuts, file links and browser bridge, and asks whether to keep your data.

    Linux packages

    DEB and RPM installs update through your package manager. The AppImage tells you when a new one is out.
